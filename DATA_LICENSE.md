# Word data licensing

The bundled learning library contains 844 practice cards: 72 original everyday-phrase cards (including 24 Spanish practical-driving-test instruction cards), 64 original padel and tennis vocabulary/phrase cards, 48 edited dialogue cards, 300 common-word/expression cards, and 360 conjugation cards.

Frequency ordering for the common-word teaching selection was informed by `wordfreq`:

- wordfreq: https://github.com/rspeer/wordfreq
- Software license: Apache-2.0
- Redistributable word-frequency data: CC BY-SA 4.0
- wordfreq attribution and source list: https://github.com/rspeer/wordfreq/blob/master/CREDITS.md

Spanish lexical forms and conjugations were checked against the Kaikki machine-readable Spanish dictionary, extracted from English Wiktionary with Wiktextract.

- Kaikki Spanish dictionary: https://kaikki.org/dictionary/Spanish/index.html
- English Wiktionary copyright terms: https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/Wiktionary:Copyrights
- Relevant upstream terms: CC BY-SA 4.0 and GFDL
- Verification date recorded in the data: 2026-08-14

The 72 everyday phrases, Chinese and English learning glosses, example sentences, CEFR labels and scene groupings were created or editorially assigned for Teclea Español. The everyday phrases are original project content released under GPL-3.0; names, dates and addresses are teaching examples rather than user data. The scope of the 24 driving-test cards was informed by public DGT practical-test materials, but their bilingual teaching text is original and is not represented as a fixed nationwide examiner script. CEFR labels are learning-order suggestions, not official certifications. These modifications and additions do not imply endorsement by wordfreq, Wiktionary, Kaikki, DGT or their contributors.

The 64 padel and tennis cards are original bilingual teaching selections. Padel court, service and scoring terminology was checked against the public FIP/FEP rules; tennis service, court and `let` terminology was checked against the public RFET/ITF rules. The rules were used as factual references and were not copied as a commercial word list or course. This does not imply endorsement by FIP, FEP, RFET or ITF.

The application code remains available under GPL-3.0 as stated in `LICENSE`. This notice preserves the attribution and licensing information for the lexical-data portion.
